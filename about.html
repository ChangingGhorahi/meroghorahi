Our Journey

Our Story: From a Question to a Movement

The Spark It began at a Youth Festival. Suzal Adhikari asked a politician: "If I were to stand for Mayor one day, what obstacles would I face?" That moment stayed with him.
The Reality Check He started documenting issues and submitting complaints. The result? Silence. The bureaucracy was deaf to lone citizens.
"The government isn't moved by rants—it's moved by legal obligation."
The Turning Point By citing specific laws, he forced action on local issues. He proved the system works, but requires legal knowledge.
Building the Engine He teamed with Shubham Devkota to build Mero Ghorahi—a system that turns complaints into legal notifications.
Today, Mero Ghorahi bridges the gap between citizens and government.
