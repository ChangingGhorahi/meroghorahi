Our Journey / हाम्रो यात्रा

From a Question
to a Movement

The Spark / सुरुवात
A Question at a Youth Festival

Suzal Adhikari asked a politician: "If I were to stand for Mayor one day, what obstacles would I face?" That question ignited everything that followed.

The Reality Check / यथार्थ
Silence from the System

He documented issues, filed complaints, knocked on doors. The response? Silence. A bureaucracy deaf to citizens without connections.

"The government isn't moved by rants — it's moved by legal obligation."

एक्लो आवाज सुनिन्न, सामूहिक आवाज सुनिन्छ। A lone voice goes unheard; a collective voice cannot be ignored.
The Turning Point / परिवर्तन
Law as the Lever

By citing Nepal's constitution and legal statutes directly, he forced accountability on local issues. The system works — but it demands legal literacy most citizens never had access to.

Building the Engine / निर्माण
Partnership with Shubham

He partnered with Shubham Devkota to build Mero Ghorahi — a platform that converts ordinary complaints into legally-backed government notifications.

Today / आज
The Bridge is Built

Mero Ghorahi bridges the gap between citizens and government. Accountability is no longer a privilege — it belongs to every resident of Ghorahi.

Your Power / तपाईंको अधिकार

तपाईंको कर, तपाईंको मत, तपाईंको पैसा —
यो सबै तपाईंकै हो। Your taxes, your vote, your money — it all belongs to you.
